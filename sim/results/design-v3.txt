design grid for yeno_bot v3 exits (mechanics only, not edge)
simulator: this commit's sim/yeno_sim.py (an open position at market change ends the run; "cliff" =
fading liquidity near close). Variants were copies of intermediate yeno_bot.py with constants patched;
"stops" = runs ended by a position open at market change. hold/cyc = mean seconds from BUY fill to flat.

variant key
  v2        committed submission-v1 bot (stops 0.10/share fee-inclusive, entries 70-90 s, flatten 60 s)
  nobids    SELL (latched) when the held side has no bids
  thin1/2   SELL (latched) when bids cover < 1x / 2x the position
  hold20/10 maximum hold 20 s / 10 s
  stop05    fee-inclusive stop at 0.05/share instead of 0.10
  floor30   SELL when the best bid < 0.30
  edepth3   entry needs bids for 3x the shares
  early75/90 forced exit from 75/90 s, entries (F+10, F+30] s
  A = nobids+thin1+hold20; B = nobids+thin2+hold10; C = B+edepth3; D = C+floor30
  B5/B7 = B with fee-inclusive stop 0.05/0.07; d2 = entry depth 2x; e90 etc. = earlier window
  v3        stage 4: B5e90 + entry depth 2x + entry immediate-loss limit tied to the stop
  v3-moveNc stage 5: final shape, stop on an N-cent gross price move (fees excluded), entry spread
            must be inside it; v3-move3c is the shipped yeno_bot.py

reading
  * Stages 1-2: every exit rule cut cliff stops (7 -> 2 of 32) by selling while bids still existed;
    none could fix thin-expiry stops, which are BUYs that filled just after the 90 s regime switch
    into a book with no bids for the rest of the market. Only moving the whole window before that
    switch (e90) removed them.
  * Stage 3 (256 stress runs): v2 38 stops, B5 16, B5e75 18, B5e90 10.
  * Stage 4-5: a fee-inclusive 0.05 stop is price-level dependent (at a 0.50 ask the fees alone pass
    it), so the stop became a gross price move. 2c and 3c both give 10 stops; 3c is kept because it
    still allows entries on a 2-tick spread (real spreads are unknown).
